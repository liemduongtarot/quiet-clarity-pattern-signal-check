Controller for this specimen: PSC application operator. No external processor is connected. Any future hosting/model/payment processor is unappointed and requires the infrastructure/commerce admission contract. Durations below are engineering bounds only. OPEN means **OPEN — SHORTEST OPERATIONALLY SUFFICIENT PERIOD TO BE QUALIFIED**, never an invented legal period.
